FAQ

How accurate is the price estimate? The estimate reflects current market trends for natural and lab‑grown stones, using a model that tracks resale percentages (natural 25‑40 %, lab‑grown 5‑12 %). It is not a formal appraisal.

Can I use the calculator on my own website? Yes. CaratBase offers free widgets (e.g., gemstone calculator) that you can embed with a “Powered by CaratBase” line. For a widget without attribution, the Widget Pro plan is $12 /month per domain.

Do I need to create an account to keep my results? No. You can store calculations on your device via My Vault without signing up.
