Ring size conversion

UK ring size V½ in US and European sizes

UK ring size V½ is US size 11 and European (ISO) size 65. The inside diameter is 20.68 mm and the circumference 64.97 mm.

The UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand share this letter scale. Europe uses the circumference in millimetres directly, which is why the European size and the circumference are the same figure.

How to check this is your size

The surest way is to measure a ring that already fits the right finger. Lay it flat and measure straight across the inside of the band. If it reads about 20.68 mm (0.814 in), you are a UK V½.

If you have no ring to hand, wrap a strip of paper around the base of the finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length. About 64.97 mm (2.56 in) means UK V½. Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest — a ring sized on a cold morning will feel tight by evening.

UK V½ is in the most common range for men's rings; US 9 to 10½ covers most men.

One half size is a very small step: going up to 11.5 (UK W½) adds 1.25 mm of circumference, and down to 10.5 (UK U½) removes 1.51 mm. Quarter sizes exist and a good jeweller will make one; if you sit between two half sizes, choose the smaller for a narrow band and the larger for a wide one.

Adjusting for the band

A wide band touches more of the finger and feels tighter at the same measurement, and a comfort-fit band (domed on the inside) feels looser. For a finger that measures 64.97 mm:
