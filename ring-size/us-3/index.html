US ring size 3 in UK, Europe and India

US ring size 3 is UK size F, European (ISO) size 44.1 and Indian/Japanese size 4. That is an inside diameter of 14.05 mm and an inside circumference of 44.14 mm.

How to check this is your size

The surest way is to measure a ring that already fits the right finger. Lay it flat and measure straight across the inside of the band. If it reads about 14.05 mm (0.553 in), you are a US 3.

If you have no ring to hand, wrap a strip of paper around the base of the finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length. About 44.14 mm (1.74 in) means US 3. Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest — a ring sized on a cold morning will feel tight by evening.

US 3 is a small size — common for a child, a pinky ring, or a very slim hand.

Adjusting for the band

A wide band touches more of the finger and feels tighter at the same measurement, and a comfort-fit band (domed on the inside) feels looser. For a finger that measures 44.14 mm:
