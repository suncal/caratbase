US ring size 9.5 in UK, Europe and India

US ring size 9.5 is UK size S½, European (ISO) size 61 and Indian/Japanese size 19. That is an inside diameter of 19.41 mm and an inside circumference of 60.98 mm.

How to check this is your size

The surest way is to measure a ring that already fits the right finger. Lay it flat and measure straight across the inside of the band. If it reads about 19.41 mm (0.764 in), you are a US 9.5.

If you have no ring to hand, wrap a strip of paper around the base of the finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length. About 60.98 mm (2.40 in) means US 9.5. Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest — a ring sized on a cold morning will feel tight by evening.

US 9.5 is in the most common range for men's rings; US 9 to 10½ covers most men.

One half size is a very small step: going up to 10 (UK T½) adds 1.35 mm of circumference, and down to 9 (UK R½) removes 1.45 mm. Quarter sizes exist and a good jeweller will make one; if you sit between two half sizes, choose the smaller for a narrow band and the larger for a wide one.

Adjusting for the band

A wide band touches more of the finger and feels tighter at the same measurement, and a comfort-fit band (domed on the inside) feels looser. For a finger that measures 60.98 mm:
