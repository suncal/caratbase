Ring size conversion

US ring size 12.5 in UK, Europe and India

US ring size 12.5 is UK size Y½, European (ISO) size 68.8 and Indian/Japanese size 26. That is an inside diameter of 21.89 mm and an inside circumference of 68.77 mm.

How to check this is your size

The surest way is to measure a ring that already fits the right finger. Lay it flat and measure straight across the inside of the band. If it reads about 21.89 mm (0.862 in), you are a US 12.5.

If you have no ring to hand, wrap a strip of paper around the base of the finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length. About 68.77 mm (2.71 in) means US 12.5. Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest — a ring sized on a cold morning will feel tight by evening.

US 12.5 is a large size, usually a man's ring finger or a thumb ring.

One half size is a very small step: going up to 13 (UK Z½) adds 1.38 mm of circumference, and down to 12 (UK X½) removes 1.26 mm. Quarter sizes exist and a good jeweller will make one; if you sit between two half sizes, choose the smaller for a narrow band and the larger for a wide one.

Adjusting for the band

A wide band touches more of the finger and feels tighter at the same measurement, and a comfort-fit band (domed on the inside) feels looser. For a finger that measures 68.77 mm:
