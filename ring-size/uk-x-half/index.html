Ring size conversion

UK ring size X½ in US and European sizes

UK ring size X½ is US size 12 and European (ISO) size 67.5. The inside diameter is 21.49 mm and the circumference 67.51 mm.

The UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand share this letter scale. Europe uses the circumference in millimetres directly, which is why the European size and the circumference are the same figure.

How to check this is your size

The surest way is to measure a ring that already fits the right finger. Lay it flat and measure straight across the inside of the band. If it reads about 21.49 mm (0.846 in), you are a UK X½.

If you have no ring to hand, wrap a strip of paper around the base of the finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length. About 67.51 mm (2.66 in) means UK X½. Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest — a ring sized on a cold morning will feel tight by evening.

UK X½ is a large size, usually a man's ring finger or a thumb ring.

One half size is a very small step: going up to 12.5 (UK Y½) adds 1.26 mm of circumference, and down to 11.5 (UK W½) removes 1.29 mm. Quarter sizes exist and a good jeweller will make one; if you sit between two half sizes, choose the smaller for a narrow band and the larger for a wide one.

Adjusting for the band

A wide band touches more of the finger and feels tighter at the same measurement, and a comfort-fit band (domed on the inside) feels looser. For a finger that measures 67.51 mm:
