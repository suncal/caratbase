Ring size conversion

UK ring size G½ in US and European sizes

UK ring size G½ is US size 3.75 and European (ISO) size 46. The inside diameter is 14.65 mm and the circumference 46.02 mm.

The UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand share this letter scale. Europe uses the circumference in millimetres directly, which is why the European size and the circumference are the same figure.

How to check this is your size

The surest way is to measure a ring that already fits the right finger. Lay it flat and measure straight across the inside of the band. If it reads about 14.65 mm (0.577 in), you are a UK G½.

If you have no ring to hand, wrap a strip of paper around the base of the finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length. About 46.02 mm (1.81 in) means UK G½. Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest — a ring sized on a cold morning will feel tight by evening.

UK G½ is a small size — common for a child, a pinky ring, or a very slim hand.

One half size is a very small step: going up to 4.25 (UK H½) adds 1.29 mm of circumference, and down to 3.25 (UK F½) removes 1.25 mm. Quarter sizes exist and a good jeweller will make one; if you sit between two half sizes, choose the smaller for a narrow band and the larger for a wide one.

Adjusting for the band

A wide band touches more of the finger and feels tighter at the same measurement, and a comfort-fit band (domed on the inside) feels looser. For a finger that measures 46.02 mm:
