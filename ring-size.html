Getting it right the first time

Measure at the end of the day

Fingers swell as the day goes on and in warm weather. A ring sized first thing on a cold morning will feel tight by evening. Measure warm and late.

Check the knuckle

The ring has to pass over your knuckle and then sit without spinning. If your knuckle is much wider than the base of the finger, size to the knuckle and have the band fitted with sizing beads.

Wide bands run tight

A band over about 6 mm wide touches more of your finger and feels snugger at the same measurement. Go up a quarter to a half size.

Buying a surprise?

Borrow a ring she already wears on that finger and measure the inside diameter. Failing that, US 6 to 6½ is the most common women's size and US 9 to 10 the most common men's. Size up rather than down — a ring can be made smaller far more easily than larger.
