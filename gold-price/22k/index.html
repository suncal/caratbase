22K gold price per gram today

22K gold is worth about $128.95 per gram at today's spot price. 22K is 91.6% pure gold, so a gram of it contains 0.916 g of gold and the rest is alloy.

A scrap buyer will realistically offer $90.27–$116.06 per gram, because they carry the cost of refining and take a margin.

What 22K is

The standard for Indian, Middle Eastern and much East Asian jewellery. Rich colour, but soft — stones are rarely set in it. In 10 g of 22K there is 9.16 g of gold and 0.84 g of alloy — usually copper and silver, which is what sets the colour: more copper reads rose, more silver reads pale, and white gold is 22k gold alloyed with palladium or nickel and plated with rhodium.

Why a buyer pays less than this

The figure above is the metal content at spot. Spot is the price of pure, refined gold traded in bulk; your ring is an alloy, often with stones and solder in it. A buyer refines it, carries that cost, and takes a margin. An offer of 70–90% of the calculated value is normal. Below about 60%, walk away.

Scrap value is the floor, not the answer

If the piece holds a diamond, carries a maker's mark, or has any antique interest, scrap is the worst price it can fetch. Melting an Art Deco setting for its metal destroys most of what it was worth.
