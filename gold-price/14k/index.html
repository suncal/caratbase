14K gold price per gram today

14K gold is worth about $84.10 per gram at today's spot price. 14K is 58.5% pure gold, so a gram of it contains 0.585 g of gold and the rest is alloy.

A scrap buyer will realistically offer $58.87–$75.69 per gram, because they carry the cost of refining and take a margin.

What 14K is

The dominant standard in the United States. Harder-wearing than 18K and noticeably cheaper per gram. In 10 g of 14K there is 5.85 g of gold and 4.15 g of alloy — usually copper and silver, which is what sets the colour: more copper reads rose, more silver reads pale, and white gold is 14k gold alloyed with palladium or nickel and plated with rhodium.

Why a buyer pays less than this

The figure above is the metal content at spot. Spot is the price of pure, refined gold traded in bulk; your ring is an alloy, often with stones and solder in it. A buyer refines it, carries that cost, and takes a margin. An offer of 70–90% of the calculated value is normal. Below about 60%, walk away.

Scrap value is the floor, not the answer

If the piece holds a diamond, carries a maker's mark, or has any antique interest, scrap is the worst price it can fetch. Melting an Art Deco setting for its metal destroys most of what it was worth.
