9K gold price per gram today

9K gold is worth about $52.79 per gram at today's spot price. 9K is 37.5% pure gold, so a gram of it contains 0.375 g of gold and the rest is alloy.

A scrap buyer will realistically offer $36.95–$47.51 per gram, because they carry the cost of refining and take a margin.

What 9K is

The UK and Commonwealth entry-level standard, not legally "gold" in the US. Pale in colour and very hard-wearing. In 10 g of 9K there is 3.75 g of gold and 6.25 g of alloy — usually copper and silver, which is what sets the colour: more copper reads rose, more silver reads pale, and white gold is 9k gold alloyed with palladium or nickel and plated with rhodium.

Why a buyer pays less than this

The figure above is the metal content at spot. Spot is the price of pure, refined gold traded in bulk; your ring is an alloy, often with stones and solder in it. A buyer refines it, carries that cost, and takes a margin. An offer of 70–90% of the calculated value is normal. Below about 60%, walk away.

Scrap value is the floor, not the answer

If the piece holds a diamond, carries a maker's mark, or has any antique interest, scrap is the worst price it can fetch. Melting an Art Deco setting for its metal destroys most of what it was worth.
