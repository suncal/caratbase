Where the money actually goes

Above roughly G colour and VS2 clarity, almost nothing you are paying for is visible without a loupe. A D colour, VVS1 stone and a G colour, VS2 stone look identical on a hand, yet the first costs far more — which is why the same budget buys so much less of it.

Cut is the exception, and the one grade worth protecting. A poorly cut diamond leaks light and looks dull no matter how good its other grades are. If something has to give, give on colour and clarity long before cut.

Remember the resale column

Every option above shows what it would fetch secondhand as well as what it costs. That spread is not a criticism of buying jewellery — it is simply the number nobody tells you at the counter. Value a specific stone for a figure matched to its exact specification.
