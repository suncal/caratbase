Size reference

What a carat actually looks like

A carat is a measure of weight, not size — 0.20 grams. Because weight rises with the cube of the diameter, doubling the carat does not come close to doubling how big a stone looks. Every diamond below is drawn to true scale on an average 17 mm ring finger.

Shape changes everything

The same carat, ten different sizes

Carat is weight, not size. Elongated cuts carry less of that weight hidden in the pavilion and spread wider across the finger, so they look considerably bigger than a round of identical weight — and cost the same or less.

Dimensions assume well-cut stones of standard proportions. Individual stones vary, and two diamonds of the same shape and weight can differ by half a millimetre or more.

Diameters assume standard proportions. A deep-cut stone of the same weight looks smaller face-up; a shallow one looks larger but loses brilliance. This is why two 1 carat diamonds can look noticeably different sizes.
