Why retail and resale are so far apart

A jeweller's retail price carries the shop's rent, staff, insurance, marketing and margin. When you sell, none of that comes back to you. A buyer is purchasing stock they must re-cut, re-set, hold and resell, so they price to protect their own margin.

For natural diamonds that typically means an offer somewhere between a quarter and two fifths of the original retail price. Certification helps — a GIA report removes the buyer's grading risk, and stones without paperwork are discounted further.

The lab-grown question

Lab-grown diamonds are chemically and optically real diamonds. The difference is supply: production scaled enormously and retail prices fell hard. The secondary market has been slower still, so resale on lab-grown stones is currently very weak. If you bought one to wear, that changes nothing. If you bought one expecting to recover value later, it matters a great deal.
