The number nobody publishes

A diamond loses most of its value the moment you buy it

Every jeweller will tell you the retail price. Almost none will tell you what happens when you try to sell it back. On the secondary market a natural diamond typically recovers only a quarter to two fifths of what was paid for it.

Lab-grown stones are a harder story again. Production scaled, prices collapsed, and resale has followed. That is not an argument against buying one — it is an argument for knowing the number before you need it.
