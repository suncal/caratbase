Pay Per Lead for Jewelers

CaratBase sends leads to independent jewelers, estate buyers, and appraisers who want customers looking to sell gold, get an appraisal, or purchase an engagement ring. The first 3 leads are free, then each accepted lead costs $40.

How the Pay-Per-Lead Model Works

You pay $40 only for leads you accept, after the first 3 free leads.
