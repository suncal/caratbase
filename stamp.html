What does that stamp mean?

Nearly every piece of real jewellery carries a mark — usually inside the band, on a clasp, or on the back. Type it in and find out what the metal is, how pure it is, and whether the piece is worth anything by weight.

The three stamps that disappoint people most

No metal value

EPNS · nickel silver · German silver · alpaca

None of these contain any silver at all. The word describes the colour, not the metal. This is the single most common source of disappointment in inherited jewellery.

No metal value

GP · GEP · HGE — plated

A base metal core with a microscopically thin gold layer. "Heavy gold electroplate" is marketing language, not a standard. Refiners will not buy it.

Low but real value

GF — gold filled

Genuinely different from plating: a bonded gold layer roughly 100 times thicker. A stamp like "1/20 12K GF" means one twentieth of the total weight is 12K gold. Some refiners buy it in bulk.
