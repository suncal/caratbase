3 carat oval diamond: size, price and what it really resells for

A 3 carat oval diamond measures about 11.11 × 8.22 mm face-up and costs roughly $25,800–$33,425 at retail for a G colour, VS2, well-cut stone.

If you sold it, you would realistically be offered $7,325–$11,725 — about 35% of what it cost. A lab-grown stone of the same size and grade costs about $4,138.

How big it actually looks

11.11 × 8.22 mm is the face-up measurement — what sits above the finger — giving a visible area of about 91 mm². Reads noticeably larger than a round of the same weight and elongates the finger. That is 4% more face-up area than a round of the same weight.

Carat is a measure of weight, not size, and weight rises with the cube of the length. Doubling the carat makes a stone only about 1.26 times longer, so a 6 carat oval would be roughly 14.0 mm across, not 22.2.

The price step just below 3 carat

A 2.95 carat oval of the same grade costs about $21,525 — 27% less than this stone — and is only 0.6% shorter across, a difference no one can see on a finger. Weights just under a round number are priced in a lower bracket because the round number itself is what buyers ask for.

The lab-grown figure is not a typo. Lab-grown prices fell by roughly 85% between 2022 and 2025 and resale is 5–12% of retail, so the saving is real at the counter and is gone the day after. Buy lab-grown to wear it; never as a store of value.
