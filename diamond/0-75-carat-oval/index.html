0.75 carat oval diamond: size, price and what it really resells for

A 0.75 carat oval diamond measures about 7 × 5.18 mm face-up and costs roughly $1,900–$2,475 at retail for a G colour, VS2, well-cut stone.

If you sold it, you would realistically be offered $550–$875 — about 35% of what it cost. A lab-grown stone of the same size and grade costs about $312.

How big it actually looks

7 × 5.18 mm is the face-up measurement — what sits above the finger — giving a visible area of about 36 mm². Reads noticeably larger than a round of the same weight and elongates the finger. That is 4% more face-up area than a round of the same weight.

Carat is a measure of weight, not size, and weight rises with the cube of the length. Doubling the carat makes a stone only about 1.26 times longer, so a 1.5 carat oval would be roughly 8.8 mm across, not 14.0.

Above about G colour and VS2 clarity, almost nothing you pay for is visible without a loupe. Cut is the one grade worth protecting: a badly cut oval looks dull whatever else is true of it.

The price step just below 0.75 carat

There is no useful saving just below this weight: a 0.7 carat oval costs about $2,038, only 7% less, and the next price bracket does not begin until further down.

The lab-grown figure is not a typo. Lab-grown prices fell by roughly 85% between 2022 and 2025 and resale is 5–12% of retail, so the saving is real at the counter and is gone the day after. Buy lab-grown to wear it; never as a store of value.
