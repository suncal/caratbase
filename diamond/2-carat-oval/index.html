2 carat oval diamond: size, price and what it really resells for

A 2 carat oval diamond measures about 9.7 × 7.18 mm face-up and costs roughly $12,725–$16,475 at retail for a G colour, VS2, well-cut stone.

If you sold it, you would realistically be offered $3,625–$5,775 — about 35% of what it cost.

How big it actually looks

9.7 × 7.18 mm is the face-up measurement — what sits above the finger. Reads noticeably larger than a round of the same weight and elongates the finger.

Carat is a measure of weight, not size, and weight rises with the cube of the diameter. That is why doubling the carat does not come close to doubling how big a stone looks: a 4 carat stone is only about 1.26 times longer than this one.

Price and the resale gap

RetailResale
2 ct oval, G / VS2 $12,725–$16,475 $3,625–$5,775

The gap is not a criticism of buying jewellery — it is simply the number nobody mentions at the counter. A jeweller's price carries rent, staff, insurance and margin, and none of that comes back to you when you sell.

What changes this figure

Colour and clarity move it most. Dropping from G to J colour and VS2 to SI2 buys a noticeably larger stone for the same money, and above about G and VS2 almost nothing you pay for is visible without a loupe. Cut is the exception and the one grade worth protecting: a badly cut diamond looks dull whatever else is true of it.
