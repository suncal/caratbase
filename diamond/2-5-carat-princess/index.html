2.5 carat princess diamond: size, price and what it really resells for

A 2.5 carat princess diamond measures about 7.46 × 7.46 mm face-up and costs roughly $14,025–$18,175 at retail for a G colour, VS2, well-cut stone.

If you sold it, you would realistically be offered $3,975–$6,375 — about 35% of what it cost. A lab-grown stone of the same size and grade costs about $2,238.

How big it actually looks

7.46 × 7.46 mm is the face-up measurement — what sits above the finger — giving a visible area of about 56 mm². Deep, so a lot of the weight is hidden below. Small face-up for its carat. That is 28% less face-up area than a round of the same weight.

Carat is a measure of weight, not size, and weight rises with the cube of the length. Doubling the carat makes a stone only about 1.26 times longer, so a 5 carat princess would be roughly 9.4 mm across, not 14.9.

Above about G colour and VS2 clarity, almost nothing you pay for is visible without a loupe. Cut is the one grade worth protecting: a badly cut princess looks dull whatever else is true of it.

The price step just below 2.5 carat

There is no useful saving just below this weight: a 2.45 carat princess costs about $15,775, only 2% less, and the next price bracket does not begin until further down.

The lab-grown figure is not a typo. Lab-grown prices fell by roughly 85% between 2022 and 2025 and resale is 5–12% of retail, so the saving is real at the counter and is gone the day after. Buy lab-grown to wear it; never as a store of value.
