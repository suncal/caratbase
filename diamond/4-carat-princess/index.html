4 carat princess diamond: size, price and what it really resells for

A 4 carat princess diamond measures about 8.73 × 8.73 mm face-up and costs roughly $36,950–$47,875 at retail for a G colour, VS2, well-cut stone.

If you sold it, you would realistically be offered $10,500–$16,800 — about 35% of what it cost. A lab-grown stone of the same size and grade costs about $5,912.

How big it actually looks

8.73 × 8.73 mm is the face-up measurement — what sits above the finger — giving a visible area of about 76 mm². Deep, so a lot of the weight is hidden below. Small face-up for its carat. That is 28% less face-up area than a round of the same weight.

Carat is a measure of weight, not size, and weight rises with the cube of the length. Doubling the carat makes a stone only about 1.26 times longer, so a 8 carat princess would be roughly 11.0 mm across, not 17.5.

The price step just below 4 carat

A 3.95 carat princess of the same grade costs about $34,412 — 19% less than this stone — and is only 0.4% shorter across, a difference no one can see on a finger. Weights just under a round number are priced in a lower bracket because the round number itself is what buyers ask for.

The lab-grown figure is not a typo. Lab-grown prices fell by roughly 85% between 2022 and 2025 and resale is 5–12% of retail, so the saving is real at the counter and is gone the day after. Buy lab-grown to wear it; never as a store of value.
